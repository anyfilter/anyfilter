# Privacy policy

AnyFilter has no servers and collects nothing.

**What leaves your browser.** To score a post, the extension sends its text, the author's name and handle, and, for replies, the post being answered, to the provider you picked in Settings: Vercel AI Gateway or TypeSafe. Nothing else is sent anywhere. What those providers do with requests is covered by their own policies: [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy), [TypeSafe](https://typesafe.ai).

**What stays on your device.** Your API key, your settings, the list of hidden posts and the counters are stored in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. They are never uploaded.

**What we see.** Nothing. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no account.

**Deleting your data.** Settings → Data → Clear everything removes hidden posts, counters and cached scores. Uninstalling the extension removes everything, including the key.

**Changes.** This file is the policy. Its history is in the repository.
